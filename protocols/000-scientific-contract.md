# Scientific contract — 10 October 2026

Working title: *Where Does Evolvability Reside? Organisms, Encounters, and
Ecological History in Self-Reproducing Cellular Automata*.
Target: a reproducible ALIFE 2027 or GECCO 2027 submission; this checkpoint is
evidence building, not a claim of publication readiness.

H1: evolved organisms can retain increased beneficial-heritable-variant yield
under a common held-out panel relative to matched ancestors. H2: yield can
depend on organism-by-partner interactions, including rank reversals. H3:
ecological history can change inherited organisms and encounter distributions.
These hypotheses are compatible. History is upstream of the full present
state; deterministic identical complete initial states have identical futures.
No residual historical effect is claimed after fixing that state.

Evolvability means the capacity to generate useful heritable variation under a
specified panel and assay. Population growth and ordinary adaptation do not
establish recursive self-improvement. H1 and H3 remain untested here.

First experiment: a small crossed focal-by-partner encounter atlas (H2).
Exploration verifies reproduction, identifies naturally generated viable
organisms, and selects workable geometry. Before the main batch, version and
freeze panel, phases, positions, horizons, tracking, inheritance criterion,
benefit assay, and analysis in protocol 001 and a JSON configuration. Preserve
unsuccessful and unresolved runs. Do not select encounters on their outcomes.

Definitions: a changed structure is not necessarily a changed genotype;
closed loops are structural observations, not automatically viable organisms.
Candidate signal genotypes require isolation reproduction to establish
viability; inheritance requires the same signal genotype in an isolated
descendant and its reproducing descendant. Benefit requires a predeclared
paired ecological assay and independent confirmation. Transient structures
remain observable so their later stable descendants can enter this funnel.

Ancestry is conservative: spatial continuity tracks structures, causal labels
can identify mixing but do not prove genetic parentage. Ambiguous ancestry is
explicit; novel genotypes after contact are pair-level outcomes unless stronger
evidence exists. Candidates are compared against both inputs for benefit.

Sampling units are distinct complete configurations in a declared finite
panel. Exact deterministic reruns are checks, not replicates. Main and held-out
configuration subsets are separated. Interaction contrasts jointly include
focal and partner contributions; a partner main effect does not establish H2.
No population-level uncertainty or generalization beyond the panel without
independent organism/history replication.
