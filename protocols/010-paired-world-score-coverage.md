# Prospective calibration of paired-world population-score coverage

EXP-score-coverage-001. Version 1, 10 October 2026. Configuration:
`configs/coverage-v1.json`; freeze receipt: `data/coverage-v1/manifest.json`.
This freezes a diagnostic design before new native continuation, root selection,
or isolation recovery acquisition. The source worlds and their structural score
records have already been observed. This is prospective for recovery outcomes,
not a blind selection of previously unobserved worlds.

## Question and scientific scope

A closed core is a detected rectangular loop body, not automatically a viable
organism. Protocol 009 counted cores only when their operational composite
identity (core dimensions and phase-normalized channel word) belonged to its
fixed registry. That identity preserves spacing and chirality but is not a full
organism state or a published species assignment. Valid identities outside the
registry and bodies with unknown identity occurred in the response worlds, while
the separate calibration selected none. Does a prospectively selected omitted
observation produce descendants with a stably transmitted identity after complete
component isolation?

Native continuation describes the structural fate of that observation in its
ecology. Isolation tests recoverability under an imposed assay. A transient may
produce a stable descendant with a different identity, including an identity
already in the registry. Such recovery establishes recoverability of an omitted
source observation; it does not necessarily establish novel inherited identity,
native parentage, ecological benefit, or increased evolvability.

Protocol 009's registry, integer scores, ratios, gates and benefit classifications
remain unchanged. The earlier protocol 007 proxy classification also remains
unchanged. H1/H3 require independent-population experiments; RSI remains
unestablished. No organism/history replication or causal disturbance effect is
identified by this diagnostic finite panel.

Replay each world from its exact start under unchanged synchronous nine-state
CTRBL periodic 192-square physics. At times 400,800,1200 classify detected cores
into three disjoint classes: scored (identity in the frozen registry), valid
unregistered (known composite identity outside it), and unknown identity. Within
each run/time/class select the first lexicographic (y,x,h,w) core before checking
extractability. Select at most one; retain available class count and selected
count for every stratum. Empty strata have zero available and selected counts,
not an invented root or a recovery failure. Never replace a shared, lost,
unextractable, nonproductive or lethal selection with another core.

There are 360 planned strata and at most 360 source observations. Coordinate
selection is an explicit diagnostic sample; it is not random or representative
of all omitted population. Core detector blind spots remain outside this assay.
Source class is immutable even if delayed identity changes. Record delayed
identity/class, confirmed descendant identity and registry membership separately.
Matched scored roots share world and source time, not necessarily intrinsic phase.
The inherited development/confirmation labels organize descriptive results; both
source panels are already observed and do not supply new held-out benefit tests.

## Native continuation and maturation

Observe each world every 20 updates through 2800, providing 1600 updates of native
continuation after each source time. No further native intervention is permitted.
Save complete native grids at source times and at delays 0,100,400 after each
source time, plus the final grid. Store compact sampled core records, identity,
counts, occupied cells, boundary activity, and fixed-core loss/recurrence times;
do not store exhaustive histories.

At each declared delay, target the exact source (y,x,h,w) core. Any sampled loss
at or before the delay makes that extraction ineligible, even if that coordinate
later recurs. Retain both sampled loss and recurrence; do not substitute another
core, resize the target, follow a matching word elsewhere, or infer continuity
through an observed absence. Unknown genotype with a still-detected core is not
core loss. Between-sample events remain unobserved; even continuous sampled
occupancy cannot prove organism identity or genetic ancestry.

Report endpoint identity and matching source word at other cores when the source
word is known. Unknown source words yield missing matching-word observations,
not measured zero. These are structural labels, never native offspring evidence
or lineage extinction. Overlapping windows, repeated source coordinates, and
convergent isolation paths are nested observations, not independent organisms.

## Complete extraction and bounded isolation

Use the pinned periodic detector and complete-component extraction. Retain arms
and embryos. Reject shared/multicore components, noncontractible components,
missing complete components, and snapshots with max dimension +20 >192. Seam
unwrapping requires an empty row and column and retention of the entire single-
core component. Do not repair, crop, detach an arm, or remove residual cells to
make an observation eligible. Unknown source identity does not by itself block
isolation; initialize its search identity as null.

Each eligible delay gets a separate bounded serial search: periodic 192-square
isolation grid, centered complete snapshot at orientation zero, generation
horizon1600, detection every10, at most three detached children per generation,
depth6 and at most24 generation simulations per delayed root. Existing
`expanded_search` depth-first ordering and periodic `offspring_generation`
semantics are fixed by source hashes. A detached child must be a complete
single-core component with a known identity and a core coordinate absent from
that generation's initial core set. This is operational reproduction evidence
under imposed isolation, not genetic parent assignment.

For all selected classes, accept any valid composite identity transmitted
unchanged along two consecutive isolated parent-child edges. Identity changes
before the stable streak are allowed. An unknown root must first produce a
known-identity descendant before two identity-preserving edges can accumulate.
Stop at the first accepted path. Preserve complete search graphs, initial/final
generation grids, child snapshots and full child-selection grids. Record root
reproduction separately from stable transmission; reproduction can occur without
confirmation. Saved grid extinction is an isolated-node outcome, not native
lineage extinction or proof that alternative capped paths would fail.

Offspring limits leave later children unsearched. Depth, simulation, size and
offspring limits remain explicit censoring fields. A horizon nonconfirmation is
unresolved, never a demonstrated nonviable organism. There are at most1080 delay
searches and30 control searches, or26640 generation simulations at the hard caps.
No automatic horizon or budget extension is allowed after seeing outcomes.

## Controls, recovery gate, and outcomes

Apply identical any-stable-identity endpoints, delays and budgets to scored roots
in the same run/time panel. Test all30 original complete phase patches with the
same budgets but require two transmissions of the original phase identity.
These are deterministic control checks, not new independent samples. Report the
identity-preserving subset of scored recoveries separately; this protocol's gate
does not replace protocol 009's original-identity recovery gate.

The new coverage gate requires all30 phase controls, at least9/10 of selected
scored observations overall, and at least4/5 within each starting state to
recover a stable identity at delay zero. Later delays describe maturation and
structural persistence; their loss-related ineligibility does not calibrate the
source-time assay. Count each selected
source observation once; all selected scored observations remain in the
denominator, including those with three ineligible delays. An empty state-level
scored denominator makes the gate unresolved. Compare exact integer fractions.

The primary endpoint is existence and counts of selected valid-unregistered or
unknown source observations with confirmed stable descendants at any delay.
Report per-delay results and one union success per source observation, with all
selected observations as the main denominator and eligible searches additionally.
Show class availability, empty strata, source/delay identities, native loss and
recurrence, extraction failures, root reproduction, isolated extinctions,
stable transmission, unresolved/capped paths and pending/interrupted work.
Report breakdowns by state, D/U, source condition, source time and source class.
Retain raw numerator/denominator integers; unknown and missing values are never
converted to zeros.

A verified positive path is recoverability evidence even if the overall gate
fails; flag the gate and limit rate interpretation. Zero recoveries only means
none confirmed in the declared sample and bounded search. D/U recovery contrasts
are descriptive because independently selected cores differ in identity, phase
and location. They do not estimate omitted contribution to protocol 009's score,
identify a disturbance effect on viability, or show total viable population.
No p-values, bootstrap intervals or independent-population confidence intervals.

## Benefit and subsequent comparison

No benefit assay is acquired here. Benefit remains pending for recovered omitted
descendants; their inheritance may be resolved under isolation while native
ancestry remains ambiguous. If omissions recover, develop and freeze a revised
population metric and a new paired assay before its main acquisition. Exploratory
sensitivity calculations must be separate from frozen outcomes. Never expand the
old registry retrospectively or change earlier classifications.

After coverage is assessed, separately freeze the fully crossed elapsed-age by
nonsymmetric-geometry comparison described in `docs/next-experiment.md`. It must
retain five complete representatives, both-input comparisons, exact one-third
cell-state/core depletion, paired undisturbed controls, exact per-condition
retention ratios, separate absolute advantage, and non-equivalent reserved
combinations. No age/geometry acquisition is authorized by this protocol freeze.
