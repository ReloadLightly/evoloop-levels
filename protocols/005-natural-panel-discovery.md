# Natural panel discovery, EXP-panel-discovery-002

This exploratory discovery is frozen before its new histories. Its purpose is
three distinct naturally generated, separately verified viable channel identities
for a subsequent crossed atlas; it does not test H1, H2, or H3. The original
N01 native source in EXP-discovery-001 is the first eligibility trial, reverified
under the same three-generation criterion as new identities. S13 is a documented
reference and a source organism, not counted as a naturally generated identity.

Histories, ordered stopping rule, dimensions, placements, horizons, and attempt
caps are in configs/panel-v2.json. D01 extends the original single-seed ecology
from its complete initial state. D02/D03 impose declared two-organism initial
encounters. They change no rules and inject no cell mutations. These histories
are discovery devices, not independent population replicates. All initial states
are saved before execution. Stop at three admitted identities; if the fixed
budget yields fewer, report the shortfall rather than silently changing selection.

At each 20-update sample, sort cores by canonical coordinates. Consider complete
single-core components whose phase-normalized native channel word differs from
S13 and admitted words. Attempt at most two distinct complete component hashes
per source composite identity and 32 attempts per history. Retain failures and
unexamined identities. Extraction includes arms. A seam-spanning component may
be unwrapped only when periodic joining leaves one core and at least one empty
row and column; otherwise extraction is ineligible. Detection covers rectangular
bodies smaller than half the domain in each dimension. Nonrectangular structures,
large bodies at a seam, and unsampled events remain outside measurement scope.
Legacy frozen experiments retain their original detector and boundary policy.

In a 192-square empty periodic arena, inspect the first detached offspring within
1600 updates, then require two further consecutive transmissions of that child's
composite identity. Sampling is every 10 updates. The stable channel word must
match the native source word; a stable composite shape obtained only after
isolation is labelled assay-derived morphological stabilization. Reject a stable
word already in the panel. Use the third detached descendant as the uniform
panel snapshot. Reject before admission unless elapsed phases 20, 50, and 80
contain one core of the stable composite identity, no occupied edge, and fit the
isolation arena. Selection never uses subsequent crossed-encounter outcomes.
Serial isolation is an imposed assay intervention, not a native ancestry claim.

Save source time/core, complete grid, extraction box, snapshots, all generation
initial/final/selection states, hashes, rule and code hashes, and phase states.
Stream metrics and capture world frames every 200 updates; no exhaustive history.
One worker; record measured elapsed time and peak RSS. Retain 200 MiB memory
headroom. Full deterministic replay and extraction comparisons are checks, not
independent evidence. No beneficial-variant claim arises from panel discovery.
