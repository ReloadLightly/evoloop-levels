# Fresh crossed atlas, EXP-atlas-002

## Question and panel

This prospective H2 experiment asks whether production and stable transmission
of variation depend on organism combinations beyond the original S13/N01 panel.
Use V01–V03, three distinct naturally generated channel identities selected and
verified under protocol 005 before looking at atlas outcomes. V01's native word
was previously discovered; V02/V03 are new. Their uniform input snapshots are
third detached descendants after two consecutive identical composite-identity
transmissions. Discovery is exploratory selection, not independent population
replication. Full discovery provenance and phase states are retained.

## Measurement and transmission

The unchanged original nine-state synchronous CTRBL physics is pinned by rule
and code hashes. New periodic measurement joins occupied components at both
seams and unwraps only complete single-core components with an empty row and
column. Four cuts recognize rectangular bodies smaller than half the domain
in each dimension; larger seam-crossing and nonrectangular structures remain
outside validated scope. Core changes and sampled losses are structural events,
not identified births or ancestry. Complete starting maps and final states
permit deterministic replay; masks overapproximate causality, not parentage.

Primary candidates have a valid phase-normalized native channel word different
from both inputs. Keep shape-only composite changes as a separate count.
Unknown channels, shared/noncontractible/oversized structures, structural
survival, newly positioned cores, and unresolved observations are explicit.
This endpoint is conditional on the native-word candidate screen: shape-only
intermediates are counted but do not enter isolation. Novelty means different
from the two inputs, not globally new to the catalogue.
Capture the earliest two distinct complete component hashes per run/composite
identity, including arms. Oversized captures consume budget and are ineligible.
All lethal, nonproductive, and no-contact configurations remain in denominators.

Each captured candidate enters the calibrated bounded depth-first search:
first three detached newly positioned valid cores once per core tuple per
1600-update generation, ordered by detachment time, core coordinates, and hash;
six generation states of depth and at most24 simulations per root. Identity
changes reset the transmission streak. Two consecutive same-composite
transmissions confirm stability. The endpoint's word must differ from both
inputs; transient structures may reach it through a different stable descendant.
Use seam-aware generation measurement; retain every stage's complete initial,
final, and child-selection grids. Record caps and unexamined paths. Verification
reruns are reproducibility checks, not independent samples. Serial isolation is
an explicit assay intervention; pair ancestry remains ambiguous. Stable words

## Analysis and benefit

The primary yield is encounters with any confirmed novel inherited word divided
by all configurations in that split. Report native candidate yield separately,
and unextractable/censored paths as unresolved rather than demonstrated failure.
For all three-by-three tables, report focal means, partner means, and residuals
from their additive fit. Test interaction descriptively using all nine two-by-two
differences of differences, strict focal rank reversals between partner columns,
and whether each main reversal persists on held-out configurations. A partner
main effect alone does not establish H2. Pair novelty cannot identify a focal
mutational propensity. The fixed panel is a census: no pseudo-replicated binomial
intervals or p-values. Paired near-minus-separated contrasts supplement tables.
