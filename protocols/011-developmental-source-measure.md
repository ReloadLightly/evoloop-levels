# Prospective recovery-certified developmental source measure

EXP-developmental-calibration-001, version1, 10 October2026.
Configuration: configs/developmental-v1.json. Protocol010 and all earlier scores,
gates and ecological classifications remain immutable. Its RV02 delay-zero gate
failed; this design does not reinterpret that failure.

A structural registry can omit source states with unreadable identity even when
they produce already-registered stable descendants. The new measurement asks
which detected source bodies recover stable descendants under complete-component
isolation. It does not count their native offspring or total viable population.

At each complete source grid, C is every detected rectangular core. Q counts
complete, contractible, single-core components that confirm any valid identity
through two consecutive unchanged isolated transmission edges. Source identity
may be unknown or differ from the stable descendant. Each source core contributes
at most one to Q. R=C-Q retains unknown identity, shared components, size or
extraction failures, isolated extinction, nonproduction within the horizon and
all unsearched/capped paths. Nonconfirmation never proves nonviability. Report
Q,R,C, source classes, root reproduction, confirmed identities, ancestry ambiguity,
censoring and pending benefit separately. [Q,C] describes detected source bodies
potentially recoverable under the assay, not an interval for all viable organisms.
Complete components include arms; no cutting or repair makes a root eligible.
Detector blind spots remain outside this measurement.

Sampling is a census of detected cores at five declared snapshots0,400,800,1200,
1600, not a representative random population sample. Native physics remains
pinned synchronous nine-state CTRBL, periodic192 square, horizon1600. Separate
fixed-registry structural counts are recorded at0,20,...1600. Store complete
starts, five census grids, compact structural samples and isolated evidence;
no exhaustive native histories. Isolation occurs at delay zero only. Native
maturation and longer searches are not added after outcomes.

Twenty new calibration worlds cross five states with two declared preparations
and D/U twins. Ages20/110 are elapsed updates, not homologous reproductive phases.
Use the saved complete protocol009 phase states, including arms and all occupied
cells, at the newly declared nonsymmetric sites. Label colonies constructed.
Remove one entire component at time zero in D; leave U undisturbed thereafter.
Before freeze verify exact one-third occupied-cell and cell-state histogram loss,
3→2 registered cores, unchanged remaining components, and no disconnected residual
cells. Compare complete D/U starts against every prior protocol009 world under
rotation/translation; equivalent cases cannot supply new ecological contexts.
Neither source ages nor calibration outcomes supply independent populations.

Search uses unchanged192-square isolation, horizon1600, detection10, three
children per generation, depth6, at most24 simulations per root. Any stable
identity is accepted for source roots. The first128 distinct eligible input keys
within each world are admitted in chronological-time/lexicographic-core order;
all later keys remain unsearched in R. Every key consumes a local admission even
when globally cached. Repeated source observations reference a byte-identical
complete-input/rule/configuration/identity/endpoint cache and retain all their
multiplicities. Cache reuse is deterministic computation, never replication.
The cap is2560 ecological searches plus33 controls, at most62232 generation
simulations. No automatic extension is permitted. Empty snapshots are measured
zero counts with undefined resolution fraction, not recovery failures.

Controls repeat all30 original-identity protocol009 phase recoveries and the
three distinct complete unknown-source inputs recovered under protocol010,
which require any stable identity. These are software/assay controls with known
outcomes, not new independent evidence. Benchmark the first control with one
worker before main acquisition. Freeze protocol, JSON, complete preparations,
implementation/test/audit hashes and input provenance before new responses.

The new-context scored recovery gate retains90% overall and80% within each
starting state, with all scored source observations in the denominator. A
separate resolved-share gate requires Q/C≥4/5 in every state×D/U aggregate across
all ten source snapshots; empty denominators are unresolved. This is a precision
qualification, not proof of sensitivity to all viable organisms. All30 original
and3 omitted-input controls must pass. Keep each gate separate, never rescue a
failure by delaying extraction, changing denominators or adding budgets. If any
gate fails, report full diagnostics and preserve failure. A later crossed design
may still be frozen, but joint ecological benefit cannot be confirmed under a
failed calibration.

For the subsequent crossed assay, per-condition sums give retention bounds
[Q_D/C_U,C_D/Q_U], using exact integer totals and Fractions. Zero C_U makes
retention undefined; zero Q_U gives an unbounded upper endpoint. Panel bounds
average per-condition bounds, never pool totals. Robust relative advantage
requires candidate lower>both originating input upper bounds in development and
reserved panels. Absolute advantage separately requires candidate disturbed Q
mean>both input disturbed C means. Ties fail; overlapping or unbounded comparisons
remain unresolved. Calibration performs no benefit assay. Prior population
scores remain distinct. H1/H3 are untested and RSI is unestablished.
