# Pre-response exposure calibration

EXP-impact-exposure-001 is an exploratory preparation and targeting check for
the next paired ecological comparison. Commit this procedure before acquisition.
It observes no post-disturbance response scores. Previous frozen experiments
and their outcome flags remain unchanged.

Retain all five complete snapshots from ecological-v1: V01, V02, V03, RV01,
RV02, the same four-identity score registry, original synchronous nine-state
CTRBL rule, and a 192-square periodic domain. Use orientation zero throughout.
RV01/V01 share an operational identity but differ in complete state.

Autonomously grow each centered snapshot, detecting every ten updates through
update 2,000. The preparation anchor is the first observation with exactly three
registered cores, all three complete single-core occupied components, and no
unregistered or unknown recognized cores. It is a sampled population-stage
event, not a measured intrinsic cell-cycle phase or native ancestry assignment.
Record the complete anchor and states at offsets 0, 40, 80, 120, 160, and 200
updates. Record count, component eligibility, body locations, and snapshot hashes.
Stop after the last offset. Failure to reach the anchor remains explicit.

At each offset, examine four fixed directional selectors, vectors (1,2),
(-2,1), (2,-3), and (-3,-1) in (row,column) coordinates. Rank complete registered
single-core components by projection of their core top-left displacement from
the anchor's initial core, with lexicographic core tuple as the deterministic
tie-breaker. Select the largest projection. The proposed disturbance clears
every occupied cell of exactly that component, including arms; save its exact
periodic mask and pre/post grids. This is an explicitly imposed organism-removal
policy, not an unmodified CA process or fixed rectangular landscape disturbance.
Verify one registered core is removed, all others are unchanged, and record
occupied-cell and population fractions. Do not continue the disturbed states.

The main experiment will use declared early offsets with three cores and a
one-third immediate registered-population loss for every starting state. If
proposed offsets are infeasible, document preparation changes in a new prospective
main protocol before response acquisition. Do not select geometries using
future population scores or exclude either recovered identity.

Reserve distinct offsets and directional policies for confirmation. Before
scoring, reject equal or rotation/translation-equivalent complete pre-states
and effective masks across development/confirmation. Matching only nominal
coordinates is insufficient. These finite deterministic conditions are nested
within five selected organism preparations, not independent evolved histories.

Use one worker, compact uint8 grids, streamed observations and selected complete
states. Measure runtime/RSS; maintain 200 MiB available headroom. Preserve code,
rule/source hashes and all calibration outcomes. Freeze a separate response
protocol, score-recovery calibration, configuration, and analysis before the
main paired continuation batch. H1/H3 and RSI remain untested/unestablished.
