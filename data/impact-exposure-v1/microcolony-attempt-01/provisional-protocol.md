# Standardized nonsymmetric, impact-matched paired population assay

EXP-impact-calibration-001 and EXP-impact-paired-001. Prospective protocol;
configuration `configs/impact-v1.json`, complete prepared grids, source hashes,
and executable analysis are frozen before acquiring post-removal responses.

## Question and scope

Does either recovered identity retain more recognized population after a matched
loss than both originating inputs, after accounting for baseline multiplication?
This tests conditional ecological performance, not evolvability, H1, H3, or RSI.
RV01 is a recovered known identity from V02/V03; RV02 a catalogue-novel identity
from V01/V03. All five representative complete snapshots are retained. Genetic
parentage is ambiguous, so comparison against both inputs is mandatory.

Exposure-only protocol 008 found native three-core preparations eligible at just
6/30 proposed offsets (all five anchors and V03+40). It acquired no disturbed
futures. The new preparation is explicitly a constructed three-clone microcolony,
not a sampled native population or a historical transplant.

## Frozen preparation and exposure

Advance each complete detached representative alone on the original synchronous
nine-state CTRBL periodic 192-square grid for ages 0,20,40,80,100,120. These are
elapsed update ages; they are not homologous intrinsic reproductive phases.
Require exactly one original-identity core in one complete contractible occupied
component containing every occupied cell. Retain all arms and embryos. Any
ineligible phase blocks freezing rather than replacing it after seeing responses.
Store the source grid, complete component, and core offset. Place three identical
copies at the fixed core coordinates in the configuration, with orientation zero.
Require distinct single-core components, no overlap, exact copies, and all cells
accounted for. All site triangles are nonsymmetric; their coordinates and removal
indices are fixed before responses.

For each condition remove exactly the designated occupied component at t=0,
including arms. Verify three registered cores become two, the other two complete
patches are byte-identical, and exactly one third of occupied cells disappears.
This matches fractional structural impact; absolute cell counts, reproductive
value, and future ecological effects can differ. Keep an undisturbed copy of each
same prepared grid. No other intervention occurs during observation. Test complete
control AND disturbed states under every 90-degree rotation and torus translation
across development/confirmation, and record masks and cross-state equivalences.
Any effective development/confirmation equivalence blocks independent condition
confirmation; masks alone need not be geometrically distinct. This does not make
configurations independent ecological histories.

## Calibration before benefit interpretation

Continue the ten separate calibration disturbed worlds for 2800 updates. At
0,400,800,1200 choose first and last distinct lexicographic cores separately in
the fixed registry and outside it, at most two per class. Preserve ineligible
components in denominators. Reproduce after isolation with the previously verified
bounded serial search: 192-square, generation horizon1600, detection10, three
children, depth6, at most24 simulations per root, two consecutive transmissions
of the originally scored composite identity. Unscored roots accept any stable
identity and are descriptive coverage checks. Also test all30 isolated phase
patches as positive controls. Save full search graphs and raw generation grids.

Gate: all30 controls recover, at least90% of selected scored roots overall,
at least80% for each starting state, and one confirmation in each nonempty scored
stratum. Thresholds apply to selected states, not independent organisms. Native
fixed-core loss/recurrence and matching-word observations over1600 updates are
structural continuation labels, never offspring ancestry or survival proof.
Nonconfirmation is unresolved under search caps, not proven nonviability.

## Paired response and exact analysis

Complete all40 worlds: five states, two development and two reserved confirmation
conditions, disturbed and undisturbed. Horizon1600; score81 equally weighted samples
at0,20,...,1600, including initial impact and zeros. Score is number of closed cores
whose exact composite identity belongs to the frozen four-identity registry. It is
not total viable population. Record unscored/unknown cores and occupied cells.

Per condition retain integer score sums D,U; retention R=D/U, undefined if U=0.
Panel retention is the arithmetic mean of its two exact Fraction ratios, not a
ratio of pooled totals. Primary relative criterion requires candidate panel R
strictly above BOTH inputs in BOTH development and confirmation. Exact ties fail.
Separately report absolute disturbed means over162 samples per panel. The joint
calibrated population-advantage criterion requires the primary criterion AND
strictly greater absolute disturbed score than both inputs in both panels, plus
passed calibration and exposure/non-equivalence validation. Report per-condition
and panel contrasts, absolute D and U, and D-U. Better relative retention alone
can arise from a smaller baseline; D>U can indicate release from crowding.

If calibration fails, complete all paired worlds as structural diagnostics, keep
numerical criteria, and mark joint ecological confirmation and yield unknown.
No winner selection or panel revision between development and confirmation. Link
confirmed originating encounter states, if any, to36 main/18 held-out/54 total
encounters only conditionally on this new assay. Sixteen other candidate-producing
encounters retain unresolved inheritance; lack of confirmation is not true failure.
No p-values, bootstrap or confidence intervals: deterministic configurations are
finite comparison conditions, not independent population histories. H1/H3 remain
untested. Growth or recovery does not establish improved capacity for adaptive change.

## Evidence and verification

Persist full initial/control/damaged grids, removal masks, phase sources, hashes,
run IDs, all sampled counts/cores, final grids, selected roots, and bounded search
nodes. Three configuration-declared paired replays every80 updates are visualization
only; no outcome-based replay selection. Independently reconstruct preparation and
removal, replay all50 native worlds and all isolation generations, recompute exact
metrics and symmetry checks. Single CPU worker; preserve200 MiB memory headroom.
All deviations require a new version and retain failed evidence.
