# Review of the prospective recoverable-source measure

Reviewed 10 October 2026 against protocols 000, 009 and 010, the recorded
coverage results, and `docs/source_review.md`. This is a scientific design
review, not acquisition evidence. The review does not change any earlier
population score, recovery gate, ecological classification, or search outcome.

## What the revised measure establishes

At each frozen source snapshot, let C be the number of detected rectangular
cores. A source contributes one to Q only when its complete, contractible,
single-core component can be isolated at delay zero and its bounded serial
search confirms an operational identity transmitted unchanged along two
consecutive parent-child edges. Unknown source identity is allowed; its first
known descendant precedes the two stable edges. Any stable descendant identity
can qualify. The source receives one count, regardless of the number of
descendants, and its source identity remains unchanged in the record.

R = C − Q retains all other detected cores, including shared components,
unextractable components, unresolved searches, and sources whose searches are
not started under the declared cap. Report Q, R and C with source classes and
reasons, rather than presenting R as measured death or nonviability. A completed
isolation search with no confirmation remains unresolved under its limits.

The interval [Q,C] describes confirmed and potentially recoverable **detected
source bodies**. It does not bound the total number of viable organisms in the
native world. The detector can miss structures; a multicore component raises
individuality questions; and imposed isolation tests a different environment.
Consequently, this interval is an operational uncertainty envelope over the
declared detected-core inventory, not a statistical confidence interval or an
unqualified native population census. It never identifies genetic parentage,
assigns a descendant genotype retrospectively to an unknown source, or counts
isolation descendants as native offspring.

This revision addresses protocol 010's observed omission: six unknown source
observations, representing three distinct complete inputs, recovered descendants
already in the registry. Registry expansion alone would not recover unreadable
developmental states. The new measure admits such a source through evidence of
stable recovery. Fixed-registry core counts remain a separate recorded quantity.
The nine valid-unregistered sources with no stable confirmation remain
unresolved; their productive capped searches must not disappear from the account.

## Prospective calibration and controls

The calibration panel comprises 20 complete native response worlds: five
representative states, two declared elapsed ages (20 and 110), and disturbed and
undisturbed copies under the newly fixed nonsymmetric geometry for each age.
Every world is inventoried at 0, 400, 800, 1200 and 1600 completed updates. These
are constructed clone ecologies, not independent evolutionary histories. The
age–geometry pairing in calibration does not estimate separate causal age and
geometry effects; the later fully crossed panel addresses that comparison.

Using the saved complete eligible phase patches at ages 20 and 110 avoids the
known age40 V03 preparation rejection. All occupied cells, arms and embryos must
be retained. A disconnected residual cell, overlapping placement, multicore
component, or noncontractible preparation blocks the corresponding preparation;
no repair or outcome-based replacement is permissible. Verify exactly one-third
occupied-cell removal, cell-state histogram removal, and three-to-two initial
core loss before acquiring disturbed futures. Record absolute exposure as well
as its exact fraction.

The search retains the previously frozen horizon of 1600 updates, detection
every 10, three admitted children, depth six, and 24 generation simulations per
root. A failed calibration cannot trigger a silent horizon, native-delay, or
search-budget extension. Such an extension would require another prospective
version and preservation of the present results.

The 30 saved phase patches and three distinct previously recovered unknown
complete inputs supply 33 implementation recovery controls. Original phase
controls must recover their originally specified identity; unknown-input
controls use the any-stable-identity endpoint. They test faithful implementation
and reproducibility of known paths. Their reuse does not make them new samples,
nor demonstrate sensitivity in the newly prepared ecological source population.

The new-context scored recovery gate retains the earlier numerical thresholds:
at least 9/10 overall and 4/5 within each starting state, with every newly observed
scored source in the denominator and the any-stable-identity endpoint. A separate
resolved-share gate requires Q/C ≥ 4/5 for each starting-state × disturbance-status
group, using exact summed integer counts across its declared ages and snapshot
times. All 33 controls must pass as a third qualification. Every detected core
remains in the resolved-share denominator, including ineligible and unsearched
cores. A group with C = 0 has an undefined resolved share and an unresolved gate.
Nested observations of persistent bodies at different times are not independent
organisms.

This Q/C gate is a declared **resolved-share criterion**, not sensitivity against
a known set of viable parents. It is a new criterion for interval usefulness;
it does not repair or replace protocol 010's failed RV02 sensitivity gate.
Passing can still leave biologically important unresolved sources. Failure means
the new panel does not provide its required resolution: retain the intervals
and numerical contrasts as diagnostics, with joint ecological confirmation
unknown. Positive isolation paths remain evidence even when the gate fails.

## Search caps and deterministic reuse

The per-world ceiling is 128 new unique component searches. Inventory all
detected cores before applying that ceiling; no source may be removed from C
because its search is expensive, ineligible, or unstarted. Freeze the traversal
order by world, source time and lexicographic core coordinates before futures.
Cache reuse is permitted only for identical complete input states and identical
measured starting identity, rule hash and search configuration. Store the full
cache binding and a reference to its audited search evidence for every reused
observation. A shared identity word alone is insufficient for reuse.

The implementation admits the first 128 distinct eligible keys within each
world in chronological-time/lexicographic-core order. A globally cached key still
consumes its local admission. Duplicate local observations of an admitted key
reuse that key; a distinct key first appearing after the cap remains unsearched
even if another world has already cached it. This resolves resume-order and
cross-world scheduling dependence of admission. Duplicate inputs reduce execution
cost, not denominators, and never constitute independent replication. Unstarted
sources contribute to R even when their identity seems familiar.

The hard maximum is 2,560 ecological root searches plus 33 controls, or 62,232
generation simulations. It is a ceiling, not an expected workload. Benchmark
one declared control with one worker, retain timing separately, and acquire,
audit, render and pack Git sequentially while preserving 200 MiB available
memory. A cap that produces an unresolved-share failure is a scientific outcome,
not grounds for selecting a more favorable subset.

## Benefit comparison and the crossed panel

For a condition with positive undisturbed structural sum C_U, the conservative
retention lower bound is Q_D/C_U. Its upper bound is C_D/Q_U when Q_U > 0.
When Q_U = 0, retention has no finite upper bound; when C_U = 0 the denominator
is structurally absent and retention is undefined. Keep these cases explicit.
Sum the declared equally weighted snapshot counts within each condition, then
average its exact Fraction bounds across conditions. Do not pool totals across
conditions. The interval describes a sparse, declared time-window measure, not
the older assay's 81-sample score.

Robust relative advantage requires the candidate's panel lower bound to exceed
both originating inputs' panel upper bounds in development and confirmation.
Separately, robust absolute disturbed advantage requires its mean disturbed Q
to exceed both inputs' mean disturbed C in each panel. Exact ties fail strict
superiority. Overlapping bounds leave benefit unresolved; they do not establish
zero true benefit. Both-input comparisons, calibrated resolution, exact exposure,
and distinct reserved challenges are required for joint ecological confirmation.
Even passing every criterion establishes conditional ecological performance,
not increased evolvability, H1, H3, or recursive self-improvement.

The subsequent crossed freeze contains five states × four declared elapsed ages
(0, 30, 100, 120) × four layouts × D/U = 160 complete response worlds. Two original
development layouts are each crossed with every age. Two new nonsymmetric
confirmation layouts are likewise crossed with every age. Original age–layout
combinations are explicitly previously observed; new combinations test transfer
across declared configurations rather than independent populations. Reserved
complete undisturbed and disturbed states must be checked against all development
states and all 40 old response starts under torus translation and 90-degree
rotation before treating them as distinct challenges. Geometrically different
masks alone do not establish effective difference.

Holding geometry fixed while changing age separates preparation dependence from
layout dependence in this finite panel. Common elapsed ages are not homologous
reproductive phases, and layout effects do not by themselves identify a crowding
mechanism. Freeze the crossed configuration only after assessing calibration,
retaining a failed gate if necessary; acquire no crossed futures as part of this
design review. H1 and H3 still require their independent-population and transplant
comparisons under the scientific contract.

## Implementation review before acquisition

The reviewed configuration, protocol and runner implement the intended source
classification, delay-zero endpoint, local distinct-key admission, immutable
source identity and exact gate arithmetic. Cache bindings distinguish unknown
from known input identity and original-identity from any-identity controls. Source
inventory precedes eligibility and admission; nonconfirmations retain their
source units. The independent auditor reconstructs source partitions rather than
importing the runner's partition or summary helpers.

Pre-freeze review also requires complete equivalence checks against all 40 prior
starts, a benchmark receipt bound to the current manifest, and an acquisition
commit whose committed manifest bytes equal that manifest. A known failed
subgroup must dominate an unresolved subgroup when combining gate outcomes.
These checks concern faithful provenance and missingness, rather than modifying
the scientific endpoint. The independent auditor and validation suite must be
complete and hash-bound before acquisition; this prose review is not their
replacement.
