# Scientific review of the completed crossed assessment

Status: accepted

Reviewed 11 October 2026. This is an independent interpretation and publication
consistency review of EXP-age-geometry-cross-001, not another acquisition or
independent-population experiment. Inputs comprise protocols 000, 011 and 012,
the frozen configuration/design/readiness, all 160 completed world summaries,
the exact analysis and independent integrity receipt, README, manuscript,
research programme, and current PDF validation receipt. The frozen implementation
review and measurement evidence were left unchanged.

The full response auditor reports zero mismatches after checking all 160 complete
native worlds, 800 census grids, 12,960 structural samples, 950 distinct serial
searches, 3,347 executed generations and 6,236 child selection states. This review
verified its recorded result/analysis/acquisition/benchmark hashes against current
bytes. Separately, a lightweight JSON check reconstructed source partitions from
world summaries and used Python Fractions to check every one of the 80 cells,
all ten eight-condition panel means, and all 100 signed contrasts. No dense
simulation, rendering, additional test suite or search extension was performed
as part of this review.

The published source census agrees with Q=5,470, R=780 and C=6,250. Registry-scored
sources contribute 5,418/6,048 confirmations, valid-unregistered sources 4/124,
and unknown-identity sources 48/78. All source observations remain in their
class denominators. The four valid-unregistered confirmations occur at update
1600 in RV02, layout B, ages 0 and 30, in both disturbed and undisturbed twins.
They share two complete input keys with one observed 13×14 identity and recover
registered 13×13 V03. The 48 unknown-source confirmations share two complete
inputs and recover V01/RV01 or V03. Neither descendant identity relabels the
source, nor does multiplicity establish independent recovery routes or genetic
parentage. These are detected bodies recovering under imposed isolation, not
an estimate or bound for all native viable organisms.

R contains 645 searched but nonconfirming observations and 135 shared-component
exclusions. The summaries preserve 499 source references to isolated-root
extinction, 5,567 observations with root reproduction, and 5,616 with some search
censoring. These categories are not interchangeable or disjoint: a confirmed
search can still reach an offspring cap. Isolated-grid extinction is not native
lineage death; nonconfirmation does not establish nonviability. The largest local
admission inventory is 30 distinct keys, below 128, so no source is omitted by
that cap in this batch. The declared three-offspring, depth-six, 24-simulation
and 1,600-update search limits remain binding. Computation reuse retains every
source multiplicity and does not provide replication.

All 30 original-identity phase controls and three known omitted-input controls
pass. All five joint qualification gates are true, including the frozen
protocol 011 calibration, matched exposure and distinct reserved starts. The
published account explicitly separates that earlier passing qualification from
main-world recovery: 5,418/6,048 is below its numerical 90% calibration threshold,
and 4/124 valid-unregistered recovery leaves substantial unresolved coverage.
Protocol 012 declared the earlier calibration as its gate; the main observations
neither retroactively change that frozen result nor establish transferred
sensitivity or complete coverage. Protocol 010 remains failed.

Retention uses exact five-census condition totals [Q_D/C_U,C_D/Q_U]. Panel bounds
average eight individual condition Fractions rather than pooling their totals.
Absolute disturbed bounds average [Q,C] source counts over the same five times
and conditions. Both originating inputs and both panels remain required. RV01's
confirmation-only absolute positive is retained: its lower mean is 293/40=7.325,
above V02's upper 221/40=5.525 and V03's upper 287/40=7.175. Development and relative
criteria fail; RV02 meets neither panel-spanning robust criterion. Neither is
joint-confirmed. The 0/36 main, 0/18 held-out and 0/54 overall values count confirmed
originating encounters under this assay, not zero true beneficial variation.
The other 16 candidate-producing atlas encounters retain unresolved inheritance.

The signed interval checks reproduce all six published contrast rows. Retention
age contrasts are 0 positive, 0 negative and 40 including zero; layout contrasts
are 2 positive, 15 negative and 23 including zero; all 20 retention interactions
include zero. Absolute disturbed-count age contrasts are 16 positive and 24
including zero; layout contrasts are 10 positive, 14 negative and 16 including
zero; interactions are 2 negative and 18 including zero. The two negative
interactions are V03 development, [−1,−2/5] for 100−0 and [−9/5,−3/5] for 120−30.
Interaction orientation is the later-minus-earlier age effect in B minus that
in A, or F minus E. An interval wholly on one side of zero resolves a signed
contrast within the operational source envelopes, not statistical significance
or independent-population uncertainty. Inclusion of zero does not establish
absence of an age or interaction effect.

The scientific argument explains why the earlier coupled age/layout changes
motivated the full crossing, then distinguishes absolute source abundance from
paired retention. It preserves the earlier first-assay numerical proxy positive
and its unresolved distinct challenge confirmation, the subsequent matched-loss
negative joint criterion, and the failed original coverage gate. D deletes one
complete clone including its arms, removing exactly one third of occupied cells
and of each occupied-state count; its neighbours remain complete and U retains
all three. Reserved
starts are distinct effective states, while 20 development aliases remain
labeled repeats. Removed-clone position belongs to each layout, E/F do not
replicate an identical A/B geometry treatment, elapsed age is not a homologous
reproductive phase, and constructed colonies are not native history transplants.

A review clarification now names protocol 009 when discussing its age/layout
covariance and states that protocol 012 crosses the factors. The final PDF was
rebuilt and decoded after that prose-only correction; neither frozen evidence
nor numerical outcomes changed. The manuscript connects H2's encounter
comparison to the future common-panel organism and ecological-history transplants.
H1 and H3 remain untested, and population abundance, adaptation, isolated recovery
or conditional assay performance is not claimed to establish increased
evolvability, a crowding mechanism or recursive self-improvement. The contribution
remains a controlled conditional measurement funnel and its qualifications,
without claiming crossed competition, interaction-generated variation or
disturbance itself as new.

No remaining scientific or publication-consistency blocker was identified.
Acceptance concerns the recorded finite-panel account and its stated limits;
it does not establish biological benefit beyond the declared assay.
