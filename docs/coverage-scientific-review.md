# Scientific review of paired-world score coverage

Experiment EXP-score-coverage-001, protocol 010. Reviewed on 10 October 2026
against the frozen protocol, `data/coverage-v1/analysis.json` and
`data/coverage-v1/recovery.json`. This review checks the interpretation of the
recorded outcomes. The [independent replay audit](../data/coverage-v1/integrity.json)
passed with zero mismatches across all 40 native worlds, 392 isolated search
roots, 969 executed generations, 411 selected delays and 137 source observations.
The [complete-input equivalence check](../data/coverage-v1/component-equivalence.json)
also passed. No simulations were run for this scientific review.

The fixed registry score omitted source observations that can produce detached
descendants with a stably transmitted identity after complete component
isolation. Six selected sources with unknown identity confirmed this endpoint
at delay zero. Their descendants have identities already in the registry. The
narrow conclusion concerns classification of transient source observations;
there is no confirmed new inherited identity or ecological benefit.

## Outcomes and denominators

Selection covered 360 run/time/class strata in 40 deterministic worlds, retaining
223 empty strata and 137 selected observations. Each selection received three
planned isolation delays. Repeated times, maturation delays and D/U conditions
are nested descriptive observations, not independent organisms or populations.

The omitted classes together have six confirmed sources among 17 selections,
with 14 confirmed searches among 25 eligible searches and 51 planned delays.
The primary source union counts each source once. The eligible denominator is
additional information; it does not replace all selected sources or all planned
delays. Coordinate selection is diagnostic and does not estimate a population
recovery rate or the omitted contribution to the earlier integrated score.

All six unknown-source confirmations occurred at delay zero. Four of these
sources also confirmed at delays 100 and 400 after their measured identity
became registered. Searches initialized from the measured delayed identity;
the original source class remained unknown. The confirmed identities correspond
to V03, the identity shared by V01/RV01, and RV02. These names are representatives,
not unique complete organism states or published species assignments. Each
identity occurs in a D/U pair of selected unknown-source observations. The
complete-input equivalence check establishes that each pair has byte-identical
complete delay-zero components and identical isolated futures: six positive
observations correspond to three distinct complete inputs. Across all classes,
362 eligible delayed inputs reduce to 188 distinct complete inputs. This
post-acquisition description leaves the frozen observation counts unchanged;
neither the six positive observations nor the three inputs are independent
evolutionary samples or confirmed novel organisms.

## Calibration limits and negative outcomes

All 30 original-identity phase controls confirmed. Scored delay-zero recovery
was 109/120, which passes the overall 9/10 criterion. State-specific results
were V01 21/24, V02 24/24, V03 23/24, RV01 23/24 and RV02 18/24. RV02 fails the
frozen 4/5 criterion, so the coverage gate fails. The later union of 118/120
cannot replace the delay-zero calibration result. All 109 scored delay-zero
confirmations preserved the source identity. A verified omitted-source path
remains evidence of recoverability despite gate failure; rate interpretation
and coverage claims remain qualified.

Zero stable confirmations among the nine valid-unregistered sources does not
mean zero reproduction or nonviability. Three selected sources admitted detached
known-identity children, in nine of their 11 eligible delay searches. All nine
productive searches reached the 24-generation-simulation cap without a stable
confirmation, leaving unsearched branches unresolved. The other two eligible
searches observed no detached child: one reached the horizon and one isolated
root grid became extinct. Shared components and sampled target loss prevented
the remaining 16 planned searches. No exclusion is removed from the denominator.

All nine valid-unregistered and all eight unknown sources lost their exact
sampled native core during the 1600-update continuation window. Native loss
does not establish lineage extinction, and isolation success does not establish
native offspring. One unknown source, RV01-confirmation-1-U at time 1200, lost
its target at 1220 and recurred at 2640. This recurrence does not restore
continuity or eligibility at later declared delays. Unknown source words have
missing matching-word observations, not observed absence. Across all selections,
133 sampled target losses and 19 sampled recurrences remain structural labels.

## Consequence for the next comparison

Keep protocol 009's registry, exact scores, ratios, calibration and benefit
classifications unchanged. The positive paths establish recoverability under an
imposed assay; they do not establish native parentage, a disturbance effect,
total viable population, increased evolvability, or RSI. Benefit remains untested
for the recovered omitted-source descendants. H1 and H3 still require the
independent-population comparisons in the scientific contract.

Before the crossed elapsed-age by nonsymmetric-geometry main comparison, develop
and separately freeze a population metric that explicitly handles recoverable
transient states and unknown identity. Simply adding the confirmed descendant
identities to the old registry cannot fix this omission because they are already
registered. Calibrate the new metric and its recovery gate prospectively,
including RV02's current delay-zero calibration failure. Preserve a fixed-registry
structural count as a distinct measurement; counting every detected core cannot
yet be called total viable population.

The crossed comparison follows that metric assessment and its own freeze. It
must keep paired undisturbed controls, exact depletion and both-input comparisons,
while separating elapsed age from spatial geometry. No crossed acquisition or
retrospective recoding follows from this coverage batch.
