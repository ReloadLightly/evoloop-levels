# Scientific review of the developmental source calibration and crossed freeze

Reviewed 10 October 2026. Status: accepted for the stated finite-panel
interpretation. This post-acquisition review checks numerical reporting and
scientific scope; it does not replace the prospectively frozen protocol 011 or
the independent executable audits. No simulation, extraction, search limit,
gate, or outcome was changed by this review.

## Recorded results and denominator checks

All 20 new calibration worlds and 100 complete source censuses are present.
Independent aggregation of the saved source records gives Q=736 confirmed,
R=84 unresolved, and C=820 detected source observations. The class partition is
726/799 registry-scored, 0/9 valid-unregistered, and 10/12 unknown-identity
observations confirmed. Those denominators sum exactly to 820; confirmations sum
to 736. They are coordinate-and-time source observations nested within worlds,
not distinct organisms, independent populations, or repeated independent trials.

All 30 original-identity phase controls and three previously demonstrated
unknown-input controls recover. New-context scored recovery is 726/799,
exceeding the frozen 9/10 overall criterion. Every state exceeds its 4/5 scored
criterion, including RV02 at 116/127. All ten state×treatment resolved shares
exceed 4/5; the lowest is RV01-U at 95/113. Both recovery and resolved-share gates
are therefore passed in their declared new contexts. This does not overwrite
protocol 010's RV02 failure or qualify all native viable organisms.

The 84 unresolved observations comprise 74 nonconfirming source observations and
10 shared-component exclusions. The former reference 34 distinct unresolved
search keys: 22 have root extinction, eight reach the horizon without offspring,
and four are productive but unconfirmed. With source multiplicity retained,
those labels are 59 root-extinction, nine no-offspring, and six productive
observations. Three valid-unregistered observations have productive unresolved
paths. This distinction reconciles source denominators with deterministic cache
reuse; these counts do not describe 74 separately executed searches. Extinction
is an isolated-grid endpoint, not identified native lineage death, and no
nonconfirmation proves nonviability.

All ten unknown-source confirmations are five bodies in each V01 calibration-0
twin at update 1600. They share one byte-identical complete isolation patch and
recover the already-registered V01/RV01 identity. Ten source observations belong
in the inventory, but demonstrate only one distinct complete recovery input.
The source genotype remains unknown; recovered descendants do not identify
native genetic parents or retrospectively supply that genotype.

No source was unsearched under the local 128-distinct-key admission cap. The
largest admitted inventory is 26 keys in one world. The assay executes 530
generation nodes across 240 distinct complete-input/search keys, including
controls. Independent integrity verification reports zero mismatches for all 20
native histories, 100 census grids, 820 source observations, 33 controls, local
admission decisions and all executed isolation nodes. Previously completed proof
receipts reused after interruptions remain hash-bound; deterministic replay and
proof reuse are verification, not replication.

## Inference and scientific presentation

The reviewed README, manuscript methods 3.12/results 4.10/discussion, research
state, experiment programme and recorded analysis agree with these counts. They
preserve earlier classifications and the failed protocol 010 gate, retain all
nonproductive and ineligible observations, separate duplicated inputs from
source multiplicity, and report benefit as unassayed in calibration. No numerical
or scientific-interpretation blocker remains for publishing this assessment.

Q confirms source recovery after an imposed complete-component isolation assay.
R retains unresolved detected source bodies; [Q,C] is an operational uncertainty
envelope over that inventory. Detector omissions, shared-component individuality,
native ancestry and ecological assistance remain outside its resolved claim. It
does not bound total viable population. Passing a resolved-share gate qualifies
the width of this particular source envelope, rather than demonstrating universal
viability sensitivity. The sparse five-snapshot measure also differs from the
older 81-sample fixed-registry structural score. Old outcomes stay immutable.

## Crossed design and its qualification

Protocol 012 freezes all five states × ages 0/30/100/120 × layouts A/B/E/F × D/U:
160 complete starts and 80 prepared pairs. Complete arms and occupied cells are
retained; one-third occupied-cell, nonzero-state histogram and registered-core
loss are independently verified. All ages occur in every layout. A layout
includes its removed-clone site, so layout contrasts concern the complete spatial
challenge rather than a pure distance effect. Elapsed ages are not homologous
reproductive phases, and constructed colonies are not native-history transplants.

The independent design audit passes with zero mismatches and 23,920 complete
effective-state pair checks: 11,200 against 50 older impact starts and 20 new
calibration starts, plus 12,720 within the crossed panel. All 12,000 reserved
comparisons are non-equivalent under rotation and torus translation. Twenty
development aliases to previously observed protocol 009 starts are retained;
there are no recorded within-panel aliases. Reserved E/F layout contrasts differ
from A/B contrasts and do not independently replicate an identical geometry
treatment. No crossed response or benefit outcome has been acquired.

Future retention envelopes use exact per-condition bounds Q_D/C_U and C_D/Q_U,
then average eight condition bounds per panel. They do not pool totals. Zero C_U
is undefined and zero Q_U gives an unbounded upper endpoint. Certified relative
advantage requires candidate lower bounds to exceed both input upper bounds in
both panels; absolute disturbed superiority separately compares candidate Q
means with both input C means. Ties fail strict superiority, while overlapping
bounds leave biological advantage unresolved. Joint confirmation requires all
frozen calibration, exposure and non-equivalence gates. A future failed gate
must remain diagnostic rather than being rescued by changed denominators,
native delays, or larger search budgets. H1/H3 remain untested; recovery or assay
performance does not establish increased evolvability or recursive improvement.
