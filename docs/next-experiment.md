# Research programme after the completed crossed assessment

The encounter atlas recovers two pair-relative inherited outcomes, while sixteen other candidate-producing encounters retain unresolved inheritance. The first ecological proxy flag remains frozen at 1/54 with distinct challenge confirmation unresolved. The later matched-removal and fully crossed source-envelope assays confirm no joint superiority under their respective frozen criteria; these outcomes do not establish zero true beneficial variation.

The completed crossed assessment includes all 160 worlds, 800 census snapshots and 80 D/U pairs. It confirms 5,470/6,250 detected source observations and retains 780 unresolved. Thirty phase and three omitted-input controls pass; the prior protocol011 qualification remains passed and protocol010 remains failed. Four valid-unregistered and 48 unknown-identity observations recover registered descendants from two complete inputs per class. Source genotype and native parentage are not retroassigned.

The design separates nominal elapsed age from complete spatial challenge. Sixteen absolute disturbed-count age contrasts and two V03 development interactions exclude zero within the declared source envelopes. Every relative-retention age and interaction interval includes zero. Some layout contrasts resolve, but removed-clone position is part of each layout and the two panels compare different spatial challenges. RV01 exceeds both inputs in absolute disturbed count in confirmation alone; neither inherited state meets the both-input/both-panel joint criterion. H1/H3 require a different sampling unit and the transplants below, rather than more deterministic reruns of these constructed colonies.

Any follow-up recovery extension must first freeze a new protocol and configuration, retain the existing bounded unresolved observations, and label enlarged searches or denser censuses separately. It must not recode previous source counts, gates or benefit classifications. Current source sampling does not measure unsampled reproductive activity, total viable population, or native ancestry. The more consequential next causal comparison is independent-population organism and history transplantation, using preselected common encounters and a prospectively calibrated beneficial-heritable-variant assay.

H1 remains untested. Sample organisms from independently evolved populations
and compare them with matched ancestors under a common held-out encounter
panel. Change organism state while fixing the assessment encounter distribution.
Independent source populations, not related selected descendants or deterministic
reruns, supply replication. Measure beneficial-heritable-variant yield rather
than ordinary growth or adaptation alone.

H3 remains untested. Compare undisturbed histories, structured-disturbance
histories, and matched shuffled-disturbance controls. Transplant history-derived
organisms into shared encounters to hold exposure fixed; transplant shared
organisms into history-derived encounter distributions to hold organisms fixed.
Crossing these transplants tests dependence between inherited organism and
exposure pathways. Independent population histories supply replication. History
acts through present organisms, encounter states, and intervention schedules:
identical complete states and schedules have identical deterministic futures.
Ordinary growth, recovery, or assay advantage alone does not establish recursive
self-improvement or evolution of the capacity for further adaptive change.
